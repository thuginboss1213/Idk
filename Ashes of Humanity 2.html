Rotate your phone sideways to play
ASHES OF HUMANITY
VR: press “Enter VR”. Point at the menu and pull the trigger. Left stick walks, right stick snap-turns. Hold X and the left stick turns you; release X and it walks again. You can also push off surfaces with your hands. Trigger shoots, grip reloads, tap Y pauses (Settings, Leave), hold Y grabs your guns back. Your equipped gun (or the pistol) is in your dominant hand (set it in Settings); the other hand holds a pistol.
Desktop: WASD move, right-drag look, click shoots, Z aims (press again to un-aim), R reloads, click menus. Phone: left joystick moves, drag the right side to look, tap the buttons to fire, aim, reload, swap guns or open the menu.
